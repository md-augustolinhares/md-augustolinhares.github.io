How does he decide?

How I work

I want to take part in the decisions on how AI enters medicine. I stand for responsible use, in three principles.

  1. Validate before trusting.

    In the MedGemma project, a low validation error was not enough: I tested the model on images from outside the training set before trusting the number.

  2. State the limits.

    Each project says what it does not do. MedGemma has no clinical validation. The Prescription Assistant does not suggest doses or treatment decisions.

  3. Protect patient data.

    None of my projects publishes identifiable patient data.

Medical Prescription Assistant

Product · individual support tool

A web app I built for a pain point in my own work: writing each prescription by hand took about 10 minutes. I replaced writing with selecting, using pre-set templates by protocol. I designed, built, prototyped and tested it in my own use. By my estimate, the time dropped to 3 to 5 minutes per prescription.

Role
Solo, from scope to architecture
Tools

Limits. An individual support tool. It does not suggest doses or treatment decisions: it only formats the prescription the physician has already decided.
