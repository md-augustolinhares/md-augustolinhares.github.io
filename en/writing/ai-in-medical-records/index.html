This went into the medical record

Written on 19 Oct 2026.

On a recent shift, I opened the progress notes of several patients and was alarmed.

You could tell AI had written the texts. The chat header was still there: "Here is the patient's progress note".

It was copy and paste without reading, with the risk of LLM hallucination and mixed-up data still there.

The medical record is a legal document. The next physician on shift makes decisions based on it. An error there can turn into a wrong treatment decision.

I am analytical and observant in my own notes. So it was not hard to spot the flaws and the potential risk.

But it could have been worse. What if the AI had suggested an inappropriate treatment? What criterion would a colleague use to accept it?

Also, many services still have no AI governance protocol. Not even formal guidance from management on the subject.

There are powerful uses of AI. This is not one of them.

My view: AI that only takes the weight off thinking is in the wrong place. It should expand what the physician thinks. It should not make us careless.

That is why AI literacy matters, in healthcare and in any profession that handles sensitive data.

I study this every day because I want to be part of this transformation.
