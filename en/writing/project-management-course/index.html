The first of six courses

Published on LinkedIn on 8 Oct 2026 (in Portuguese).

I completed the first of six courses in the Google Project Management Certificate.

I expected to learn about deadlines and schedules. I learned something else: managing a project starts with deciding what is in, what is out and who needs to know what.

In medicine, we already think this way. A good medical history organizes the case before any treatment. A good project organizes the idea before building it.

In healthcare, my reading is that many AI solutions do not stall on technology. They stall on missing scope, owners and adoption by the people who use them.

I will apply the project life cycle, the choice of methodology and change management at EXC Medicina and in my AI-in-health projects.

Five courses to go. One at a time.
